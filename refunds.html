Refund and dispute policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains how Buvexa protects buyers and treats sellers fairly when something goes wrong with an order. It applies once Buvexa opens for orders.

How your money is held

When you pay, Buvexa holds the money. The seller is paid only after your order is delivered and you have had 2 days to report a problem. If you are happy sooner, you can tap All good to release the money early.

What is covered

  • The order did not arrive.
  • The order is not what was listed, for example the wrong item, the wrong colour or size, or a different condition.
  • The seller cancelled after you paid, or did not arrange delivery in time. In these cases we refund you automatically.
  • Delivery failed and could not be completed.

What is not covered

A change of mind is not covered at launch. If you simply no longer want an item, it is not eligible for a refund under this policy.

How to report a problem

  • Open the order in your account within 2 days of delivery and choose Report a problem.
  • Pick whether it did not arrive or is not as listed, tell us what happened and add photos if you can.
  • The seller's payout is paused straight away.

What happens next

  • The seller is told and can reply once, with their side and photos.
  • Our team reviews both sides and decides: refund you in full, refund you part of the amount, or pay the seller.
  • We aim to decide within 72 hours of your report, and we tell you and the seller the result.
  • A refund goes back through the payment provider to the person who paid. How long it takes to reach your account depends on your bank or payment provider.

Returning items

At launch we do not run automatic return shipping. If we decide an item should come back, we will tell you and the seller what to do. In some cases we may refund you and let you keep a low-value item.

Group carts

The person who paid for a group cart receives the refund. Group members settle any share between themselves outside Buvexa.

For sellers

If a problem is reported, your payout for that order is paused until it is resolved. If we decide in your favour, you are paid. If we refund the buyer, you are not paid for that part of the order. Repeated problem reports may lead to a review of your account.
