A marketplace built around people, not just products.

Most online shopping is a lonely list of items from strangers far away. Buvexa starts with who is near you and who you shop with.

You see sellers close to you first, so deliveries are quicker and cheaper. You can plan a purchase with friends in a shared cart. And every payment is held safely until the order arrives, so buying from someone new feels less risky.

For sellers, Buvexa is a free way to reach nearby buyers, take orders online, and run walk-in sales from the same dashboard.

What makes Buvexa different

Sellers near you show first

Buvexa sorts by distance, so the nearest sellers come first. You still see everything, and you see how far each seller is. Delivery is quicker and costs less when the seller is close.

We never show a seller's exact address. Buyers see only the area and the distance.

How we keep buying and selling safe

  • Payments are held, not handed over

    Buvexa holds your payment until delivery and a 2-day window have passed.

  • Sellers are verified

    Each seller confirms a phone number, shows a valid ID and a selfie, and links a bank account in their own name before they can publish.

  • Reviews come from real orders

    Only a buyer whose order was delivered can leave a review.

  • Problems are decided fairly

    The seller can reply once, then our team decides and tells both sides the result.

  • We never see your card

    Card and bank details are handled by licensed payment providers.

Good to know

  • Returns for a change of mind are not covered at launch.
  • Protection covers orders that do not arrive, are not as listed, or are cancelled by the seller.
  • Refunds go back to the person who paid. How long they take to reach your account depends on your bank or payment provider.
  • Counterfeit, stolen or illegal goods, and digital accounts or login details, are not allowed on Buvexa.

Questions people ask

What is Buvexa?
Buvexa is an online marketplace for Nigeria. You shop from sellers near you, you can build a shared cart with friends, and your payment is held until your order arrives.
Is Buvexa open yet?
Not yet. We are preparing to launch. Join the waitlist and we will email you the day it opens.
How much does it cost?
It is free to join, for buyers and sellers. Buyers pay for their items and the delivery fee shown at checkout. Sellers pay a small commission only on orders that are delivered.
How is my money protected?
When you pay, Buvexa holds your payment. The seller is not paid until your order has been delivered and you have had 2 days to report a problem. We never see your card details. Payments go through licensed payment providers.
What if my order does not arrive, or is not what was listed?
Report a problem in the app within 2 days of delivery. The seller can reply, then our team decides. If you are right, you get a refund. Read the refund and dispute policy.
Can I return something because I changed my mind?
Not at launch. Our protection covers orders that do not arrive, are not as listed, or are cancelled by the seller.
How do group carts work?
Start a group cart and invite friends from your friend list. Everyone can add items, and each addition shows in the group chat. One person pays through one ordinary checkout, and the group settles up between themselves outside Buvexa.
Who can sell on Buvexa?
Anyone aged 18 or over in Nigeria with a valid ID and a bank account in their own name. We verify each seller before they can publish products.
How do sellers get paid?
Two days after delivery, to the seller's verified bank account, minus our commission. If a buyer reports a problem, the payout waits until it is resolved.
How does delivery work?
Sellers ship with courier partners, starting with Sendbox, or deliver themselves. A self-delivered order is confirmed with a 4-digit code that only the buyer has.
Where will Buvexa operate?
Across Nigeria, starting close to home so deliveries are fast. We will announce the first cities at launch.
How is my personal data handled?
We collect only what we need to run the service and we never sell it. Read our privacy policy.
