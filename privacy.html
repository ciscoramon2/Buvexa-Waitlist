Who we are

Buvexa ("Buvexa", "we", "us") runs an online marketplace in Nigeria that connects buyers and sellers. For questions about your data, contact us at abdulwassiabulrahman@gmail.com.

What we collect

Depending on how you use Buvexa, we collect:

  • Account details: your name, phone number, email address and a hashed password.
  • Order details: the items you order, your delivery address, delivery status and payment status. We do not store your full card number. Card and bank details are handled by our payment providers.
  • Location: your area, or your precise location if you allow it, so we can show sellers near you first.
  • Seller verification details: a photo of your ID, a selfie, your bank name, account number and account name, and your shop details.
  • Messages: chats between buyers and sellers and inside group carts, and messages to our support team.
  • Device and usage data: your IP address, device type and browser, and logs of how you use the service, to keep it secure and working.

How we use your data

We use your data to:

  • Create and run your account.
  • Show you sellers and products near you.
  • Process orders, hold and release payments, and pay sellers.
  • Arrange delivery with courier partners.
  • Verify sellers and prevent fraud and abuse.
  • Send you service messages such as order updates, codes and receipts.
  • Give support and resolve problem reports.
  • Meet our legal obligations.

We send marketing messages only if you have agreed, and you can opt out at any time.

Our legal basis

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 we rely on your contract with us (to provide the service), our legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention and improving the service), legal obligations, and your consent where we ask for it, for example to use your precise location.

Who we share data with

We share only what is needed, with:

  • Payment providers, to take payments, hold funds, issue refunds and pay sellers.
  • Courier partners, who receive the seller's pickup address and your name, phone number and delivery address to deliver your order.
  • Identity and bank verification providers, to check a seller's identity and bank account name.
  • Email and SMS providers, to send you service messages and codes.
  • Hosting and technology providers who run our systems for us.
  • Sellers, who see the buyer details needed to fulfil an order, such as your first name, area and delivery address.
  • Friends in a group cart, who see your name and the items you add.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal data.

Seller ID documents

ID photos and selfies are used only to verify a seller's identity. Access is limited to staff who need it, and these documents are never shown to buyers or other sellers.

How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account is active and for as long as needed for the purposes above. Transaction and verification records may be kept longer where the law requires. When we no longer need data, we delete it or make it anonymous.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its use, or move it to another service. You can withdraw consent at any time. To use these rights, email abdulwassiabulrahman@gmail.com. If you are not happy with our response, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Keeping your data safe

We use measures such as encryption in transit, hashed passwords and limited staff access. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep your codes private.

Cookies and similar tools

We use only the cookies and browser storage needed to run the site, such as keeping you signed in. If we add analytics or advertising tools, we will update this policy and ask for consent where required.

Children

Buvexa is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Data outside Nigeria

Some of our providers may process data outside Nigeria. When this happens we take steps to keep your data protected.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will post the new version here and change the date at the top. For important changes we will tell you directly.
